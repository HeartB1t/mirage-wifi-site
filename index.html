04 Frequently asked questions

License, editions and responsible use.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free is the basic, free reconnaissance; Pro unlocks the advanced offensive modules (early access). With Free you scan networks and clients, fingerprint encryption, capture handshakes and PMKIDs and produce basic reports. With Pro you activate GPU cracking, Evil Twin, MITM, WPA3 downgrade, DoS, WPA-Enterprise, automated attack chains, PDF reports, BeEF, custom exploits and AI Advisor.

How do I get Free?

You download it for free, install it and use it with no cost and no credit card: it's meant for basic recon. At first launch a legal consent gate asks you to confirm that you'll use the tool only on authorized networks; the offensive endpoints stay locked until you unlock Pro. Pro is in early access: for now you can join the waitlist.

How much is Pro? How do I get it?

Pro is a subscription at 29 EUR/month and is in early access: it opens via a waitlist. Leave your email and we'll let you know as soon as it's available; the license is verified server-side and unlocks the advanced offensive modules.

Is MirageWiFi an open source project?

No. It's closed-source proprietary software, licensed and not sold, governed by an EULA (see the LICENSE file). The two Free and Pro editions share the same license agreement; Pro only adds the unlocking of the advanced modules.

Can I use it on any network?

No. You may use it only on networks and systems you own or for which you have obtained explicit, written authorization to test. It is dual-use security technology: unauthorized use is illegal and you are solely responsible for complying with applicable laws.

What does it run on?

It's designed for pentesting-oriented Linux, typically Kali, with Wi-Fi cards capable of monitor mode and injection. It supports multi-adapter, with per-panel attack interface selection when you use multiple cards.
