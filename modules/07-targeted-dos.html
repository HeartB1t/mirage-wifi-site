07 Destruct

Targeted DoS

Deauth and disruption aimed at a single target to force clients to re-associate and complete the capture.

Capturing a handshake sometimes needs a client to reconnect. This module applies disruption narrowly, aimed at the single target in scope rather than the surrounding airspace, so the effect stays controlled and documented.

When to use

When a capture is stalled because no client is reassociating, within the authorized target scope.

Output

Reassociation events that let the capture complete, logged against the specific target.

Value for the auditor

A controlled, scoped way to unblock capture without disturbing out-of-scope networks.

deauth targeted

Use only on networks and systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
