09 AI Advisor

Next-step suggestion

Contextual advice on the next attack step, a single step at a time: local Ollama or cloud BYOK, never the whole chain automatically.

The AI Advisor reads the current state of an assessment and suggests a sensible next step, one at a time. It runs on a local Ollama model or on your own cloud key, and it never takes the operator out of the loop by running a whole sequence on its own.

When to use

When you want a second opinion on where to go next, while keeping every decision in the operator's hands.

Output

A single suggested next step with a short rationale, never an automatic chain.

Value for the auditor

Guidance for less experienced operators without surrendering control or sending data anywhere you did not choose.

AI Ollama · cloud BYOK

Use only on networks and systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
